# Changelog

## v1.2.1

- Rebuilt for the 29 September 2026 game update (bows, ground bait, currency). The update changed `Item.LocalHit`, which made v1.2.0 throw errors every frame and broke kill aura, boss rush, presets and the startup banner. They all work again.
- **No Sway**, **Big Laser** (a laser sight 20x longer and brighter) and a **Custom Crosshair** (plus / dot / X, adjustable size, dark outline). The crosshair follows where your gun actually fires, which is the barrel direction when you hip-fire.
- **Ctrl + Insert** emergency open, and **Reset Keybinds**.
- The item list search shows match counts and has a clear-search row.
- Chat kill-feed and announcements are now a single saved setting in Settings, off by default. **Say KRAKEN in chat** still works on demand.

## v1.2.0 — KRAKEN

The first public release. The menu has been rebuilt as **KRAKEN**, an old-school COD-style modded-lobby menu.

### Menu
- COD-style menu with a blue header, selection bar, scroll bar, message feed, menu sounds and a **KRAKEN LOADED** banner.
- You select with Space and go back with Backspace, and you can walk around while the menu is open. The mouse doesn't interact with the menu.
- **Controller support:** hold LB + D-pad Up to open, D-pad to navigate, A to select, B to go back.
- Rebindable hotkeys, 3 presets, saved locations that persist between sessions, 7 menu colours, and settings for menu position and scale.
- Removed duplicate rows so every feature lives in one menu.

### Features
- Player, fishing, weapon, item spawner, teleport, vehicle, players, troll, fun, lobby, casino, world, ESP and chaos menus.
- **Silent aim** that hits fish underwater, aimbot, triggerbot, explosive bullets, no recoil, no cooldown, damage multiplier, full gun upgrade.
- Rigged roulette, guaranteed win and multiplied winnings.
- ESP with boxes and lines.
- **Chat kill-feed** that every player sees, boss spawner with **Boss Rush**, seagull army, auto-sell, kill aura, third person, free cam and spectate.
- **Sky base**, **KRAKEN water car** and **race track** with a lap timer (built locally).
- **Island teleport** buttons and **Unlock All**.
